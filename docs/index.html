A little less switching

Hindi in the script you want

Owlio can keep Hindi in Devanagari or use optional Gemini romanization to write recognized Hindi in English letters. English words stay in English. Romanization sends the recognized transcript for conversion; local audio stays on your computer when you use local speech.

AI features are optional and may send the relevant text to the provider you configure. Review output before using it.

Good to know

Common questions

Is Owlio free?

Yes. The official app is currently free, requires account sign-in, acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Notice, and basic usage reporting. No payment card is required. The Terms say future paid features would require separately disclosed pricing and your explicit approval.

Do I need to install Python?

No. The Windows installer includes the app. Owlio's guided setup downloads its private Python runtime, speech libraries and the speech model you choose.

Does Owlio send my audio to the cloud?

Local speech models process audio on your PC. If you choose a cloud speech provider, the app sends audio to that provider. AI cleanup and romanization send the relevant text to the provider you configured.

Can I use Owlio offline?

Local dictation can work offline after setup, subject to the account access check described in the Terms. The official app requires an online sign-in and access check at least once every 24 hours.

Where can I read the source and report a problem?

Visit the Owlio GitHub repository to explore source code, review releases or open an issue.
