Privacy by architecture

Clear boundaries about what leaves your PC

With local models, speech recognition happens entirely on your machine. Cloud AI providers are contacted only when you explicitly turn on a cloud feature.

Privacy Notice → Terms of Use →
  • Usage events exclude your content Basic analytics measure app health. No audio recordings, dictated text, API keys, or email addresses are included.
  • History stays on your drive Dictation logs, custom vocabulary, and drafts live in your private Windows user profile.
  • Cloud AI is strictly optional Stay fully local, or connect your own Gemini or Groq API keys when you want them.
  • Free, no payment card required No subscription fees under current terms. Any future paid add-on will need your explicit, separate approval.

Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

How many languages does Owlio support?

Over 100. The multilingual Whisper models recognize around 100 world languages with automatic detection. Bodhan Flex adds 27 regional Indic languages and dialects, with optional Roman Hinglish conversion. Accuracy varies by language and model size.

Does Owlio send my voice or text to external servers?

Not when you use local speech models. Recognition runs on your CPU or NVIDIA GPU. If you choose a cloud speech provider (such as Groq) or AI rewrites (such as Gemini), only the audio or text for that specific action is sent, using your own configuration.

Can I use Owlio completely offline?

Yes. Once your local speech model is downloaded and your account access has completed its daily check, local dictation works without an internet connection.

Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

The installers are currently unsigned, so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm. Check the SHA-256 hash of your download against the one on the official GitHub Release, and you can also read or build from the MIT-licensed source.

Do I need to install Python or other packages?

No. The installer sets up the app, and the guided setup downloads its own private, isolated Python runtime and speech model packages.

Is Owlio free and open-source?

Yes. The desktop source is MIT-licensed on GitHub. The official app is free with sign-in, acceptance of the terms, and disclosed basic reliability reporting.
