# Owlio 1.0.19

## Highlights

- Upgrades microphone diagnostics: replaces frequency-averaging with accurate Time-Domain RMS and Peak Amplitude metering.
- Adds Peak-Hold needle and smooth animation decay to prevent meters from instantaneously dropping to 0%.
- Introduces "Record 5s quality sample & listen" feature allowing users to immediately hear their recorded voice and inspect signal clarity, peak dBFS, and noise floor.
- Adds direct toggles for hardware/driver DSP: Noise Suppression, Auto Gain Control (AGC), and Echo Cancellation.
- Adds quick "Rescan mics" button to detect newly connected USB audio devices.

## Requirements

- Windows x64
- Owlio account and acceptance of the current legal documents
- Required basic usage reporting
