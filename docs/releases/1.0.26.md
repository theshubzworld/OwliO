# Owlio 1.0.26

A new look. Light and dark themes: choose Auto, Light or Dark at the bottom of the sidebar; Auto follows Windows, and the title bar follows your choice.

Four sections instead of ten. Home shows the owl's status, a one-line summary of your week and your dictations grouped by day; Copy, Correct and Play appear when you point at a dictation. Word book keeps your spellings and shortcuts on one page. Setup lists every setting on one page and walks through five steps (Speech, Text, Style, Shortcut, Account) with Previous and Next. Labs holds Meeting notes and voice rewrites, off by default; people who already saved meeting notes keep them switched on. Scratchpad and Insights were removed.

The interface was rebuilt on one design foundation: Windows' Segoe UI Variable font, one type, spacing and corner scale, flat sections instead of nested boxes, and one content width on every page. The floating owl bar is unchanged.

Recordings are now kept for seven days and then deleted; the text stays in your history. Turn recordings off in Setup if you don't want them saved.

Validation: 173 automated tests, TypeScript checks, renderer build, an Electron walkthrough of Home, Word book, Setup and Labs in light and dark, and Windows x64 installer packaging.

Installer: Owlio-Setup-1.0.26.exe.
